✨ Features

🎨

Neo-brutalist Design

A distinctive hard-border shadow style that blends modern and retro looks, with light and dark theme support.

📱

Responsive Layout

Perfectly adapts to any device, from desktop to mobile, for the best experience.

🔍

Platform Filters

Quickly filter contests by platform and view a specific platform in one click.

📋

One-click Copy

Copy full contest details instantly, including name, time, platform and more.

📧

Email Reminders

GitHub Actions can send daily contest digests by email, so you never miss a contest.

⚡

Real-time Updates

Contest data refreshes automatically every 15 minutes to stay accurate and current.
